## 3. Constructive K1 source-role proof

### 3.1 Complete source law, not a new primitive

Let X be the full source state described above. A successful P is the ordinary paid Chisel on living target t. It applies its 4/7 attack through native Strength, Weak/Cracked flooring, multipliers, sequential Block, Thorns and death, then participates in the one-card pending-chip settlement. Only a connecting attack on a still-living target, with combat live at settlement, supplies `1 + printed_chip + Beacon`. The printed chip is not an unconditional extra `kind=chip` effect.

The target mediator is a coupled state, not a new mark: chips r, next threshold q, Adamant state, staggered u and Cracked v, with the actual Ember/collateral consequences. `apply_chips` repeatedly subtracts q and increases the next q. An unspent Adamant hold may reseam without a real Shatter. A real Shatter sets u, adds two v and performs all native effects. Keep Stun AND Cracked together; no Stun-only redesign is proposed.

C is an independently playable paid Resonant Lance on its selected target t. It evaluates `u_t OR v_t>0` before its own hit and deferred chips. Its attack base is 7/10 multiplied by two only when that guard holds; ordinary Strength, Weak, Cracked scaling, multipliers, mitigation and callbacks then apply. C does not inspect a Chisel label, consume a Chisel mark, or retroactively benefit from its own later Shatter. Other sources of the guard remain genuinely available.

The mediator is target-local. Stun affects the next enemy action; Cracked decays under native ticks; partial chips and harder panes remain relevant to subsequent actions; death and new combat invalidate the old target context. These equations define the nominated source transition for every valid source state, including guards that suppress hits/settlement. They do not require a numerical payoff in unavailable continuations.

### 3.2 Nonredundancy and the smallest state-separation witnesses

A correct observable quotient cannot erase all producer progress, the consumer read, Stun, or threshold history:

1. Two equal otherwise-live states with facet 5 and remainders 2 versus 3 have the same false echo guard. The same connecting base Chisel followed by the same legal Lance produces different preconsumer coupled states and immediate Lance hits. Thus the pre-existing echo Boolean is not a sufficient state for the whole role. This is a future-continuation distinction, not a new name for that Boolean.
2. At remainder 3/facet 5, suppressing only Chisel's printed extra chip leaves the paid hit and ordinary one chip but removes this particular Shatter before C. Keeping P and disabling only Lance's special multiplier retains ordinary Cracked scaling and a nonzero hit. The plain four immediate-consumer cells (P component, C component) 00/01/10/11 are **7, 7, 10, 21**. No value is declared a native threshold or whole-command zero; the cells witness two material source dependencies.
3. A direct intervention on the joint Stun/Cracked preconsumer state, retaining paid history/resources and threshold fields in the paired constructed state, changes C's consequence. It is an intervention sensitivity witness, not a claim the edited state is an ordinary legal history.
4. States with the same true echo guard but different Stun have different next enemy-phase branches: skip versus act. Remainder/next-pane differences can also change a later threshold crossing. Retaining required observations therefore forbids a quotient that hides Stun/overflow and identifies the whole role solely by the echo guard.
5. Without P, C remains legal and deals its ordinary hit; without C, P retains its hit and possible Shatter but does not execute the nominated echo consequence. Wrong order cannot alter an earlier hit. Retargeting loses the first target's state. Resource, missing-UID and death/terminal denials are unavailable tails, not legal zero-payoff observations.

These are lower bounds on distinctions a sound quotient must retain, together with the source transition equations. They are not a claim to have enumerated the native game's complete state space. Removing the printed-chip dependency, target read or coupled Stun from the model breaks the corresponding checks. Increasing a count of identical recipes would not establish any of this.

### 3.3 Genuine substitution and R3 are not hidden

An ordinary attack at one chip short can produce the same real Shatter and enable the same Lance. Direct Cracked can enable Lance without Shatter. A helpful shared background can replace the nominated producer in some states. Those facts are included in the checks, not excluded to create apparent necessity. Two ordinary attacks can sometimes accumulate Chisel's chip contribution but have different paid actions, zones/counters and settlement timing; equality of a final guard does not identify those full commands.

The role has a nonredundant producer-to-target-state dependency in an explicit source domain, not a claim that Chisel is universally necessary or uniquely capable. The same distinction permits K2's attributable Empower contribution despite other genuine Strength sources. Whether the complete nominated MEC meets the bound **native** proper-subset/causal and behavioural contract is still an admission question that may fail.

R3 `COMMAND_CONTRACT_FAIL` remains closed.
