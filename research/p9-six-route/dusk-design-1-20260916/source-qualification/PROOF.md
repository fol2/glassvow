### 4.1 Complete source interface

### 4.2 Nonredundancy and independent consumer

A producer-present and producer-absent state with the same legal Flurry and otherwise same non-Strength context lead to different hit transitions in a live, unclipped state. Therefore removing the Strength mediator/read from the role system does not preserve observable behaviour. The producer can also be present without a consumer, producing status but no hit; the consumer alone remains legal and produces its base hits. Reversing the order does not change past hits. Retargeting preserves the player mediator while a target-local status does not follow it. Repeated producer increments cannot be collapsed to a nonstacking readiness bit; repeated consumers do not consume it.

More importantly, the repeated-read consumer is not simply a renamed single damage operation. Its per-hit floor, sequential Block/death/Thorns and once-per-card chip interfaces are retained. A scalar equal to a selected three-hit total fails on interruption and rounding contexts. Splitting it into three paid cards changes legal availability, card/Attack counts, settlement, discard and callbacks. Those are part of the canonical observation, not erased carriers.

These arguments establish a real, source-expressible producer/visible mediator/independently enabled consumer interface. They do not establish that the policy is good, that the cards are naturally acquired often enough, or that all native subset comparisons pass.
