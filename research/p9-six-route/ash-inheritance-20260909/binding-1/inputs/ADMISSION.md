## First-acquisition state-lock result

The corrected inventory reproduced hand-size support on structural null: 35
active, 93 inactive, 41 final-pair reachable and 35 consumer-reached policies.
The hand-size-plus-Poison inventory nevertheless failed because Poison was
active for 109 policies and inactive for only 19, while functional separation
left only four hand-size-only policies. The proposed state-lock capacity screen
also failed with only one hand-size-token-only policy.

The deterministic boundary was therefore to close the first-acquisition lock
before implementation. No knob, threshold or simulator row was created.

## Ash package-pair result

Bloodfire was not acceptance-mandated and its earlier preference-filtered
commitment grammar failed. That did not negate its already clear exact-candidate
causal complementarity or its unfiltered whole-run support. A separate
preregistered zero-row audit therefore compared hand-size and Bloodfire on the
same complete structural-null policy rectangle:

| Package | Active | Inactive | Reachable | Consumer reached |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hand-size payoff | 35 | 93 | 41 | 35 |
| Bloodfire | 36 | 92 | 57 | 36 |

Functional separation was 27 hand-size-only, 28 Bloodfire-only and eight
cross-active policies, with zero faults. Both package sensitivity, reachability
and eight-exclusive-policy gates passed. The result admits these two Ash
directions for detector construction without retuning the failed commitment
grammar.
