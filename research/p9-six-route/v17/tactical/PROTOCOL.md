## Exact observed deficiency and bounded repair

A controlled native Ash state has 6 HP, 3 energy, 6 Embers, Emberflow 1, an empty hand, and the known remaining draw multiset containing one Nova. A 20-HP enemy deals 5 damage; no omen/relic exception. Under the both.json research configuration (Nova slope8 above4, Ashfall poison1/Ward5), the old one-turn planner scores endTurn24 versus useArt51 and spends. Actual endTurn without spending yields HP1/Embers7 and a legal next-action Nova win. Spending yields HP6/Embers4/enemyHP19 and that same Nova does not win. This shows a horizon-dependent value omission, not proof that every longer Art branch loses.

The additive controller retains old action enumeration, public determinizations, two samples and twelve continuation actions. At a returned nonterminal leaf it uses the native preview only to select potentially fight-ending enemy/all-enemy cards, checks legal energy/targets, executes the candidate in an isolated public-state clone, and credits a terminal only when native result is win and player HP positive. No preview, resource formula or route label alone can grant a win. Unsupported/unrecognised immediate wins fall back to the old heuristic; this is not complete planning or an optimality claim.

`leaf_terminal=false` returns the old function/action/value exactly on the tested state. The extension is not in the current shortcut screen. It does not mutate gameplay laws or read live hidden order/RNG. The per-run research telemetry adds decision query, native rollout, terminal probe and hit counts.
