# Issue #421 progress — existing cadence and reward-overchip closures

## Decision

Close both remaining source-defined existing mechanisms without implementation:
Executioner's Seal's authored tenth-Attack cadence and the complete reward-pool
Attack overchip route. Both are preregistered decision boundary 2 results.
Issue #421 remains open and continues with a materially different structural
mechanism.

## Executioner's Seal cadence

Current-main source fixed the Dusk-favoured relic set before support was read.
Shatterer's Crown was already closed and Prism Charm is immutable #524
evidence, leaving Executioner's Seal as the sole untested existing relic in
that set. The upper-capacity condition deliberately combined final ownership
with any fight containing at least ten Attack plays; it could only overstate a
real proc.

Only 11 of 64 policies robustly owned the relic, six met that permissive
potential-activation condition and four were viable potential-active policies.
The frozen minima were 16, 16 and eight respectively. Candidate-only
separation also reached only three policies. Exact acquisition/proc telemetry
was therefore not justified. The cadence, ownership, rarity, score and offer
path remain unchanged.

## Reward-Attack Facet overchip

The source filter exhaustively selected the two natural reward-pool Attacks
with authored positive top-level chip: Bellstrike (`oblivionStrike`) and
Ringing Blow (`uppercut`). Starter Chisel was frozen as a broadness control and
could not enter the candidate. Current-main source assertions also fixed the
normal connecting-Attack chip calculation and the native overflow rule that
retains surplus chips against the next harder pane.

The necessary-capacity condition was deliberately permissive: a candidate
card play and any Shatter in the same fight. It does not claim the card hit,
caused the Shatter or carried overflow. The route passed reachability,
viability, source breadth and two-sided Scoreline separation, but was already
potential-active for 41 of 64 policies and left only nine exact-inactive
policies against the frozen minimum of 16. It is therefore another broad
Facet-pressure route rather than an identifiable repertoire discriminator.
No exact chip telemetry or causal factor was added.

## Frozen design and budgets

The overchip preregistration is explicitly a single-cell immutable
observational reuse, not a factorial. Scoreline payoff and Afterimage payoff
are separate causal factors, each fixed at current. Acquisition priority,
Faultline rarity and Ward setup priority are also fixed. There is no causal
contrast, interaction estimate or joint-cell expansion.
