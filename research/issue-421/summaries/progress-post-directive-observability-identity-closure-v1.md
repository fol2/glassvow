# Issue #421 progress: observability selection and identity closure

## Outcome

The complete zero-row observability-gap audit selected `upgrade-completion` as
the only one of eleven frozen current-main transition surfaces eligible for an
identity-safe telemetry preflight. The preflight then failed its exact identity
gate and closed this observation implementation at decision boundary 2. It did
not authorise a capacity screen, payoff, causal factorial, content candidate,
ML, RL, optimiser, protected seed or product change.

This is an archive-only scientific result under issue #421. It is not a product
branch, PR, acceptance claim or P9 receipt.

## Zero-row observability audit

The protocol froze the complete transition catalogue, source assertions,
mechanical list-derived counts, prior-closure aliases, separate Scoreline and
Afterimage package factors, package anchors, estimands, gates and all three stop
rules before reading the cached trace schema. It read at most 512 cached rows,
opened no simulator process, allowed zero new simulator rows, zero ledger rows,
30 seconds, zero model-context tokens and zero protected seeds.

`upgrade-completion` alone passed all six gates: it is unclosed, has an
observability gap, has two natural completion paths, has three direct policy
controls, needs four fields and has one deterministic successful-completion
hook. The other ten surfaces failed at least one frozen gate or matched an
already closed family. Deterministic Python selected the first eligible surface
by telemetry-field count, hook count and identifier. No behavioural count or
candidate judgement entered selection.

The original audit protocol incorrectly stated that `row.deck` retained
terminal upgrade flags. Exact source and cached rows show that `row.deck` is an
integer deck size and that no terminal per-card upgrade detail exists. The
original protocol and summary remain immutable. The correction was frozen in
the subsequent identity protocol before execution. It strengthens the observed
gap and changes no catalogue count, eligibility gate, selected surface or
method authority.

## Identity-safe telemetry preflight

The minimal research patch added one trailing `capture_upgrades` Boolean, one
four-field observation append after the existing successful upgrade assignment,
and one focused probe. It changed no content, payoff, policy, candidate or RNG
call. Exact Godot 4.7.1 asset import, focused parse and diff checks passed before
the first simulator row.

The preregistration froze 64 policy identities and simulation seeds
346596-346599 in two matched arms: 256 explicit-null rows and 256 enabled rows.
Both arms used the same frozen content, policy identities, seed identities,
first-look budget, estimator and stopping rule. The cap was 512 new simulator
rows, zero ledger rows, 120 seconds, zero model-context tokens and zero protected
seeds. No cohort, seed, field, hook or row could be added after first look.

The one execution finished in 25.09 seconds. It observed 302 rest upgrades, 72
event upgrades and 76 enabled rows with no upgrade. All event schemas and UID
checks passed, explicit null emitted no telemetry, the immutable reliability
allowance passed, and the two new arms were pairwise exact after removing the
observation. The required old-baseline comparisons nevertheless failed in 223
of 256 identities for both explicit null and enabled-with-observation-removed.
The frozen futility rule therefore rejected the telemetry implementation. It
will not be repaired, rerun or reused for capacity or payoff claims.

## Post-execution provenance audit

The first read-only diagnostic preserved the correct boundary-2 rejection but
made an internally contradicted root-cause claim: it said all non-package fields
were exact while reporting 198 non-package mismatch rows. It is preserved
unchanged as evidence of that diagnostic defect.

The zero-row v2 audit supersedes only that interpretation. It found 198
non-package mismatch rows and 186 RNG mismatch rows. Differences included deck,
deck IDs, economy, fights, gold, HP, relics and 24 outcome transitions. Policy
vectors remained exact, and explicit-null versus enabled remained exact.

The reused baseline was the current-content arm of the frozen Ward research
experiment, executed with research-modified `BalancePilot` and `BalanceSim`
blobs. It is valid for contrasts that froze those research implementations, but
it is not a pristine current-main simulator identity anchor. Consequently the
strict identity failure is genuine. Pairwise equality of the two new arms does
not reverse the preregistered rejection.

## Frozen disposition

- Close this upgrade telemetry implementation without repair or rerun.
- Make no capacity, package-separation, payoff, causal or product inference from
  its 512 rows.
- Treat earlier cached screens as frozen research-simulator evidence where that
  is the implementation they actually fixed, not as pristine current-main
  simulator identity evidence.
- Keep Scoreline payoff and Afterimage payoff separate. Neither payoff varied in
  this preflight, so there is no factorial or interaction estimate.
- Add no ML, RL or optimiser: the selected deterministic observation route
  failed identity before it could establish decision value for another method.
- Before another simulator row, perform a bounded zero-row provenance and
  authority audit to identify the next structurally valid current-main boundary.
