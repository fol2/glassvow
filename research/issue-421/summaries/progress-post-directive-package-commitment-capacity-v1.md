Close the normalised-payoff `policyPackageCommitment` grammar without

## Preregistered question

The zero-row audit asked whether the frozen root-550 structural-null reward and
economy outcomes contained enough necessary capacity for a deterministic
within-aspect commitment rule. The rule compared Scoreline and Afterimage, and
Bloodfire and Poison, only after separately dividing their payoff weights by the
four geometric medians frozen before the earlier identity preflight. It fitted
no sign, threshold, normaliser or outcome tie-break.

The strict-retention oracle was explicitly not a causal intervention estimate.
A pass could authorise only implementation and an exact null/intended-mediator
identity preflight.
