# Issue #421 immutable retained Dusk inventory

## Decision

Close the immutable #524 retained Duskblade edge inventory as a source of the
missing package and continue to a materially different evidence-backed
mechanism inside #421. Do not retune or splice either the Ward or Kindle
families.

This is decision boundary 2 from a preregistered zero-new-row necessary-capacity
audit. It is not a detector, content, product or protected-seed decision.

## Immutable scope

The exact #524 package gate retained nine local causal edge-aspect
interactions. Only three were Duskblade interactions:

1. `offering -> verdantBranch` in `kindle-draw`;
2. `brace -> fortify` in `ward-double`;
3. `bulwark -> fortify` in `ward-double`.

The first was excluded before any count because immutable #525 exhausted the
Kindle/Branch scalar, one-card and primitive ladder with sufficient held-out
precision. The exact #525 Ward level-2 packet was already rejected on
current-main whole-run evidence. This audit therefore allowed no content or
reward change; it could only ask whether the two unchanged current-main Ward
edges already had sufficient whole-run capacity.

Issues #524 and #525 were read only through their exact immutable blobs. Their
historical plans were not reconstructed and none of their experiment rows was
rerun.

## Frozen zero-row design

The only data source was the already complete current-main baseline output
`110a70a2df530ab1ef6d2f992bda7d6041a02ccf18a9d2a683aaded140da02bf`:

- root 551 policies 0-63;
- Duskblade Vow 5;
- simulation seeds 346596-346599;
- 256 existing policy rows;
- zero new simulator rows, zero ledger rows, 60 seconds maximum wall time,
  zero model-context tokens during execution and zero protected seeds.

For each edge, activation required a final deck containing the producer and
Mirrorlight, both cards played and positive `wardDoubledByFortify` in at least
one of the four simulations. The frozen necessary-capacity gates were 16 active,
16 inactive, eight reachable and eight consumer-reached policy identities with
zero faults. If both edges passed, Bulwark preceded Brace because immutable
#524 measured the higher legacy acquisition probability before these support
counts were read.

## Result

Neither unchanged edge came close to the active-policy minimum:

| Current-main edge | Active | Inactive | Reachable | Consumer reached | Both offered |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Brace -> Mirrorlight | 2 | 62 | 13 | 2 | 61 |
| Bulwark -> Mirrorlight | 2 | 62 | 3 | 2 | 45 |

Brace activated only for policies 35 and 62. Bulwark activated only for
policies 15 and 53. Their activation sets did not overlap. Current-main
Scoreline again had zero active policies, so neither edge could establish the
missing Dusk functional pair.

One baseline row was an actual 30-turn Rootheart stall:
`ward-discovery-policy-baseline-duskblade-19-346599`. That reliability witness
also fails the zero-fault gate, but it is not needed for the decision: each
edge had only 2/64 active policies against the frozen minimum of 16, and
Bulwark reachability was 3/64 against eight.

The deterministic runner therefore selected no producer and returned
`close-immutable-retained-dusk-inventory`. The ledger remained byte-identical
at 479,669 records, SHA-256
`d96ee5162d5c7878f1e4b394791632e4cd514153c1510f4e8d055cbc4cb9834e`,
SQLite integrity `ok`, protected rows 0.

## Scientific boundary and next action

Every Dusk edge retained by immutable #524 is now either excluded by #525's
precision closure or below current-main whole-run necessary capacity. This
does not say that no Duskblade mechanism exists. It says that another Ward or
Kindle iteration would be adaptive rescue rather than evidence-backed
narrowing.

The next cheapest scientific step is to freeze a deterministic source-and-trace
screen over materially different authored current-main mediators. The screen
must exclude every tested #524 package and every #525/closed #421 family before
reading support counts, require an existing measurable producer-consumer
mediator and sufficient current-main co-occurrence, and select at most one
mechanism for a causal prototype. No ML/RL or optimiser is justified yet.
