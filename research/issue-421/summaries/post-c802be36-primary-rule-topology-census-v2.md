# Post-c802be36 primary-rule topology census v2

## Boundary

This zero-row method was preregistered in [comment
5467242621](https://github.com/fol2/glassvow/issues/421#issuecomment-5467242621).
Its sole same-source extraction correction was frozen in [comment
5467251381](https://github.com/fol2/glassvow/issues/421#issuecomment-5467251381).

The method asked which player-controlled combat transition topology keys absent
from current-main Glassvow occurred in at least two independent official rules
systems and survived immutable closure. It did not author or test a Glassvow
candidate.

## Retrieval

Six frozen searches inspected ten ranked results. Q2 had no eligible official
English complete rules document in its first five results and was not replaced.
Q1, Q3, Q4, Q5 and Q6 supplied five eligible games from five publishers, above
the frozen four-document/three-publisher minimum.

The v1 web PDF extractor failed before semantic coding on its fixed content-size
ceiling. Correction v2 retained every query, rank, game and URL and changed only
the extractor to exact-URL `curl` plus `pdftotext -layout`. Source and extracted
text identities are frozen in the retrieval record. The correction used the
method's complete 1/1 budget.

## Coding fault and disposition

H1 received Q1, Q4 and Q6 under the frozen alternating assignment. All hashes
matched. Those extracts total 67,159 whitespace-delimited words; H1's initial
rules retrieval exceeded the 70,000 model source-token ceiling before a
topology could be admissibly coded. It stopped with
`INCONCLUSIVE_SOURCE_CONTEXT_CAP` and no topology.

The owner immediately interrupted H2 before it returned any topology because
one mandatory report had already made the method inconclusive. H2 output is not
used. No owner consolidation or ranking occurred.

Final disposition:

`INCONCLUSIVE_PRIMARY_RULE_TOPOLOGY_CENSUS_AT_CORRECTION_CAP`

This is an unavailable method gate, not evidence for or against any transition,
C5, strategy package, product choice or P9 acceptance claim. No semantic result
from the retrieved rulebooks may be selected adaptively from this failed
method.

semantic endpoints cannot be reused as if this census had succeeded. A distinct
safe method may build a blind finite literal lexicon from the already frozen
