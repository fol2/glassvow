## Exhaustive acquisition result

The preceding exact-source audit left Novaflare as the sole uncovered native
Dusk carrier. Its normal card-reward selector then failed independent
Afterimage support and is already closed. Current-main source contains exactly
two remaining arbitrary-card acquisition channels that bypass normal reward
decline: Library's five-card event choice and the shop's one rare-card slot.

Both were preregistered as separate mechanism blocks over the same immutable
64-policy by four-seed rectangle. They were not combined with the normal reward
route or each other, and they did not form a factorial.

The deliberately optimistic shop block used the lowest possible current rare
price, 101, and ignored Merchant's Mark ownership, gold, competing stock and
purchase order. Even that upper bound was potential-active for 56/64 policies
and left only 8 exact inactive against the frozen minimum of 16. It also left
only 5 Scoreline-only and 7 Afterimage-only policies against 8.

The event block asked only whether Novaflare had a positive-probability Library
offer ordering in which it ranked highest, and treated any event node as a
Library opportunity. That upper bound was potential-active for 55/64 policies
and left only 9 exact inactive. It likewise left 5 Scoreline-only and 7
Afterimage-only policies; its Afterimage Jaccard of 0.7581 also exceeded the
0.75 ceiling.

Both blocks had 64 robust path-opportunity policies and 20 viable
potential-active policies. Their failures therefore identify broad acquisition
geometry and cross-package saturation, not missing reachability or viability.
