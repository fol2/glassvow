## Causal rather than storage partition

For a deterministic fight-local mediator, a producer predicate has exactly one
of three origins:

1. one current-main state transition or event;
2. a deterministic relation or arithmetic transform over current-main
   transitions; or
3. a genuinely new product-semantic transition.

A relation over existing transitions has one of the smallest finite forms
relevant to a single bounded mediator: order/lifecycle, target or instance
identity, or arithmetic threshold/precision/excess/residual. A larger history
predicate is not lexicographically smaller than these forms and needs an
additional semantic selector unless exact source supplies one. A consumer is
likewise an existing transition or a new semantic transition. The six rows
below therefore partition causal topology, not merely storage location.
