remains in the frozen legal grammar after the reshuffle closure.

the mutually exclusive algebraic cells `killingBlow=true && overkill==0` and
`killingBlow=true && overkill>0`. That observation is not scientifically closed,
but it is also not an authorised next mechanism:

1. its only frozen observation audit stopped inconclusive before selecting any

   a producer-subset rescue of the closed complete post-kill consumer topology;

   telemetry, not a decision-valued experiment.

The current boundary is therefore an **unavailable/inconclusive relevant
mechanism-selection gate**, not another routine mechanism choice. This is a
bounded statement about the exact current source, trace grammar and immutable
decisions. It is not a claim that every imaginable Duskblade mechanic is
impossible.

## The residual terminal-hit surface

The source-defined exact-lethal and positive-overkill cells are materially
different *observations* from target transfer, Shatter and authored target scope.
They are also finer than `DIE`: they distinguish zero excess from positive
excess on the canonical hit that caused death.

They are not valid positive evidence yet. Protocol
`protocols/post-v30-unused-discrete-transition-audit-v1.json` (SHA-256
`169fb241e556d9dd5729ae6bbbd5a6b541ab4ccb098cfa30435ed01ca0a1dc01`)
froze the two cells and reshuffle as observations, but summary
`summaries/post-v30-unused-discrete-transition-audit-v1.json` (SHA-256
`a26063c374b21063674f72e49aee06be208f2c43a3d1055b1b18d9bd6e26155d`)
stopped inconclusive on a source assertion and selected zero representations.
That result must remain immutable; the protocol's intended cells cannot be
promoted as if its gate passed.

The causal options now diverge:

- reward both cells alike: this reduces to the closed any-kill family;
- use either cell before the immediate next `PLAY`: this is a selective rescue
  of the closed complete post-kill consumer topology;
- reward exact lethal only: this authors a precision-damage product incentive;
- reward positive overkill only: this authors an excess-damage product incentive;
- hold either cell for a different/later consumer: this authors a new lifecycle
  and consumer grammar.

The last three are materially different player experiences and the evidence
does not rank them. Selecting one would therefore be product-semantic choice,
not deterministic completion of a frozen research grammar. A trace-only rerun
would not resolve that choice.
