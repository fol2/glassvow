## Canonical product terms

- **Status** is a named, stackable combatant condition with an expiry or spend
  lifecycle. A newly named status that an existing action sets and another
  existing action consumes is not C5. (`CONCEPTS.md` lines 432–441.)
- **Facet** is enemy structural integrity. Reusing its existing value as a
  predicate is C1. (`CONCEPTS.md` lines 443–446.)
- **Ward** is temporary pre-health protection, absent rather than zero and
  actor-neutral. Its chip and stone have separate ownership and clocks and may
  not be repurposed. (`CONCEPTS.md` lines 448–469.)
- **Intent** is sequencer-pushed next-turn information rather than an actor's
  inspection of global combat state. (`CONCEPTS.md` lines 471–480.)
- **Pile** and **Candle** define three fight-local card zones and a per-turn,
  non-accumulating resource budget. (`CONCEPTS.md` lines 802–816.)
- **Crack**, **Vessel** and **Death rite** are not combat-state mediators: the
  Crack recording cap is presentation-only and the rite's same-frame handoff
  is visual truth. (`CONCEPTS.md` lines 398–428.)
- **Counterfactual self** is a closed inverted-kit concept; an unknown kit is a
  fault, not a free selector. (`CONCEPTS.md` lines 482–487.)

## Necessary C5 dimensions

1. Producer and consumer must be materially new combat-local semantic
   transitions rather than a renamed storage field or existing action.
2. State must be bounded to one fight and have explicit ownership,
   lifecycle/expiry, reset and deterministic transition order.
3. The causal contract must freeze producer, mediator, consumer, bounded
   payoff, natural cost, Dusk boundary, separate Scoreline and Afterimage
   factors, interference, exact null, policy exposure and direct activation
   objective before rows.
4. Status, Ward, Intent, Pile, Candle, Facet and visual-only terms retain their
   defined meanings. UI timing cannot substitute for gameplay causality.
5. Card, target, pile, intent, damage, Ward or death consequences remain
   commercially legible.
6. A written non-alias proof against every closed topology and Ward-spend is
   required before protocol freeze.
