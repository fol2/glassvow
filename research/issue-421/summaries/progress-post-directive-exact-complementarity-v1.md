101 of 128 policies and activated it in 94, so reward-path reachability is not
the immediate blocker. The blocked experiment found near-zero Afterimage
payoff effect but positive adjacent Ward-setup-priority effects. The current W0

another payoff value, another Ward scalar or another observed scalar cell.

package-order grammar: when a registered producer and consumer are co-held and
the mediator is absent, sequence the producer first. It must apply equally to
all registered pairs and undergo null identity proof before new rows.

ML/RL is still deferred. A repertoire bottleneck and decision value are now
measured, but the deterministic structural grammar is the smaller causal
method. A more complex optimiser requires that mechanism to fail plus a new
method-comparison justification.
