The Sunken City

A drowned city of blue glass and quiet stone. Raised causeways carry the journey between a roofless library and flooded reading courts.

Act II · Step 3 chapter review

This review is for the chapter’s assets, scale, composition, roads and water. The images and film below come from the native 3D scene. Select any image to inspect it at a larger size.

01 / Architecture & scale

A city with depth

The library is the principal landmark. Smaller civic groups combine towers, reading wings and low flooded courts. Four submerged quarters suggest the wider city beneath the water. Bridges have structural arches and usable space beneath them.

02 / Water & materials

The flood belongs to the buildings

Moving surface ripples distort the reflected architecture. Lower courts meet the same water level as the surrounding city, while the causeways stay dry. Large stone flags keep the ground quiet.

Eight seconds captured from this native candidate. The same water fills the courtyard and the surrounding city; moving ripples distort reflections of the architecture.
