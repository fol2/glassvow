# Rejected experiments

- The first bounded courtyard was superseded by the owner’s explicit path-in-void direction.
- A realtime sky shader made radial fans under the orthographic camera. It was removed in favour of a single canvas haze layer.
- The obsidian echo foot initially intercepted the usable approach. Its placement was moved onto the widening; the unchanged floor/headroom gate now passes.
- The generated combat ledge baked its checkerboard into RGB. It was not adopted; the existing alpha-bearing ledge supplies coverage.
- A kit material initially enabled back-face culling on the two-sided glass. Native inspection exposed missing triangular panes; retaining the source two-sided treatment fixed them.
- The first two MovieMaker attempts recorded 1180×820 while the script resized to 1458×820, cropping the frame. The window resolution switch did not override MovieMaker’s initial viewport. The accepted recording explicitly keeps the study at 1180×820 throughout. The two preceding logs are retained here but do not qualify video framing.
- Six documentation anchors moved after the two-line combat opt-in. The anchor gate caught them; its mechanical repair is recorded separately.
