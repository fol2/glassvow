## Direction and inspection

The owner requested grandeur, emptiness, sadness and darkness after finding
the previous sanctuary too complex. The direction derives from settled story
in `docs/story/00-truth.md` sections 1, 2.4 and 2.6 and `03-acts.md` Act IV:
the monuments are walkers, the endpoint is the hearth seen from its other side,
and the final passage ends the arrangement that sent others in the player's
place. Familiar hearth light should therefore carry unease. This is an art
interpretation, not a new canonical fact or game-copy draft.

The new composition limits large structures to a window-wall, two surviving
piers and the small hearth wall. A recessed mirror court and thick broken
foundation edges define absence with physical scale. The second pass removed
an unintended branch and reduced fine surface texture. The agent inspected
these corrections, the sparse monuments, subdued lighting and retained combat
motifs, and considers the image ready for subjective owner review.

The image is concept art, not native runtime or generator evidence. It does
