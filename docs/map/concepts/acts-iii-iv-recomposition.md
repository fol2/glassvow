## Contract and review

The owner rejected Act III's repetitive stones and Act IV's flat item display.
The response is spatial recomposition: integrated buildings, different floor
levels, enclosing architecture and meaningful focal points. Calm surface
materials remain important but must not produce visually empty environments.

Act III now has an audience court, gatehouse, cloister terraces and raised hall;
the halo belongs to this precinct. Act IV contains a rose-window wall, vault
ribs, recessed mirror courts, library echoes, rooted cloister and hearth apse.
The second Act IV pass removes the front staircase and breaks a peripheral
floor to reduce alternate-route cues. The images establish architectural and
emotional direction only: exact graph geometry, navigation overlays and the
single-route Act IV gameplay contract remain later layout/runtime obligations.

The agent inspected the outputs for chapter motifs, differentiated composition,
spatial depth, landmark hierarchy, quieter surfaces and visible approaches.

## Act IV route and material correction

Refine this final-chapter sanctuary concept while preserving its wonderful substantial architecture, monumental amber rose window on left, vaulted library reflected in recessed mirror courts, central bridge, pale-rooted cloister on right and intimate hearth apse upper right. Preserve camera and colour/light mood. Correct the traversable route so ONE clear continuous journey is legible: entry is at the FAR LEFT EDGE below the big rose window, arriving along its wall promenade; the promenade curves down to pass through the broken circular obsidian arch at lower-middle-left; from that arch proceed directly along the central stone bridge to the rooted cloister on right; then the existing shallow stair leads to the upper-right hearth. This sequence must be unambiguous. Remove the FRONT-LEFT external entry staircase below the circular arch: replace it with a thick closed ruined retaining wall and a small recessed non-walkable ledge so it cannot read as a second entrance. Remove the walkable FOREGROUND-RIGHT perimeter gallery around the near mirror pit: it should be a visibly BROKEN structural remnant, with absent floor spans and closed parapet ends that make it non-traversable, while retaining arch supports and interesting enclosing mass. Do not damage the central bridge or the route from right bridge landing to hearth. Keep the far-left rose-window promenade continuous to the circular arch, leave glass visible. The final scene is one beautiful route through five spaces, not a hub with optional branches. Also simplify the TOP WALKING SURFACES ONLY to broad matte warm-grey painted stone slabs with large sparse seams and no small crack mosaic or grain. Preserve rich wall silhouettes, windows, arches, bookcases and detailed contact shadows; simplicity of path should contrast with meaningful architectural detail. Preserve calm mirror reflections, no waves. No UI, markers, arrows, text, characters or new decorative objects. This is a refined hand-painted top-down concept, not a game screenshot.
