## Owner correction

James rejected v4's alignment with combat: colour, tone, ground and buildings
all required reconsideration. Merely restoring architecture and retaining
purple glass and a halo was insufficient. The earlier agent pass was too narrow.

This proposal retains an intact court and its spatial hierarchy, but redesigns
the entrance, hall and arcade around sharp obsidian planes and Gothic glass.
Near-black violet surfaces, magenta joints and hard edge highlights replace
grey-brown masonry. Broad dark floor slabs reduce fine surface noise.
