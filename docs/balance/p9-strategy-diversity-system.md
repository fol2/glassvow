# P9 Strategy-Diversity System

**Status:** active programme method for [#421](https://github.com/fol2/glassvow/issues/421); the iOS release bar remains authoritative until a validated replacement detector is promoted.

**Purpose:** establish and continuously certify strategic plurality for the current release candidate and future balance/content changes without replaying the entire historical research programme for every new card, relic, encounter or expansion.

## 1. Product outcome

P9 is not a one-off attempt to make one content SHA pass a fragile metric. It is a reusable scientific capability that must:

1. establish at least two independently validated, functionally distinct, viable and reachable strategy packages for each aspect and gated vow;
2. distinguish strategic plurality from one-route dominance, flat overpowered content, random-build leakage, aspect reversal and global-difficulty movement;
3. preserve several distinct high-performing endpoints under optimisation rather than manufacture an archive whose entries collapse to one attractor;
4. certify the exact release content and product SHA with determinism, reliability, content validity, aspect identity, RandomBuild separation and the Vow-5 ceiling intact; and
5. support change-impact-driven recertification so later content does not automatically trigger a new research-ticket chain or a complete rediscovery campaign.

The spirit and numerical strictness of P9 are unchanged. The method changes because the historical programme showed that repeated scalar/family search, followed by a complete landscape, is not an efficient reusable content-development loop.

## 3. Core definitions

A **strategy package** is not a name, deck label or card list. It is a reproducible tuple:

`producer -> player-visible mediator -> independently enabled consumer -> bounded payoff -> expiry/reset`

plus:

- build/acquisition conditions;
- an enacted policy contract;
- trace-level activation and contribution observables;
- a real reward/economy path;
- aspect/vow scope; and
- a canonical behavioural identity after player-facing labels and implementation carriers are erased.

A package is **functionally distinct** only when all three boundaries hold:

1. **formal:** its observable transition behaviour is not equivalent to, or merely a decomposition/union of, a closed or already admitted package;
2. **causal:** intervention on the mediator and consumer changes the intended payoff through the complete chain, while proper subsets and exact-null comparators do not reproduce it; and
3. **behavioural:** repeated full-fidelity runs retain a reproducible trace descriptor and policy-sensitive activation pattern distinct from the other package.

A package is **viable** when competent policies can exploit it under the frozen quality and guardrail contract. It is **reachable** when the live reward/economy system offers and acquires it with sufficient support, or a separately validated deterministic path exists. It is **retained** only when fresh local optimisation from distinct starts produces several distinct high-performing endpoints without archive protection forcing artificial occupancy.

## 4. Scientific architecture

The programme has five ordered layers. A later layer cannot rescue a failure in an earlier claim.

### Layer A — representation and bounded synthesis

Use the current source, immutable closed-family evidence and the commercial/product constraints to freeze a finite typed grammar. Specify required and forbidden observable traces before implementing a named mechanic.

For each candidate topology:

- prove source/runtime feasibility, bounded lifecycle, player visibility, controllability and exact null behaviour when absent/off and on the other aspect;
- erase names, IDs and implementation carriers;
- minimise the observable transition model and compare it with the closed-family quotient library;
- reject relabels, redundant state, unions of closed mechanisms and candidates whose only distinction is an unobservable carrier;
- use counterexamples to eliminate the invalid family, not only one instance; and
- return either one canonical survivor, at most three predeclared rivals for decisive discrimination, or a scoped no-survivor/unrealizability receipt.

Current frontier: every authorised minimum causal class and additive minimum has a valid closure. The immediate route is one separately frozen non-minimum campaign with at most three zero-row topologies. Existing exact negatives remain closed.

### Layer B — causal admission and descriptor validation

A formally novel topology is not yet a strategy. Freeze producer/mediator/consumer interventions and exact A/B/AB or factorial controls. Require:

- producer relevance to the mediator;
- direct mediator intervention changing consumer eligibility/consequence;
- consumer removal eliminating the payoff;
- proper subsets failing to reproduce the complete effect;
- exact-null behaviour on absent/off and the other aspect;
- no global-power, RandomBuild, stall or duration movement masquerading as package value; and
- independent policy/seed reproduction.

Derive the package descriptor from realised traces, not from its authored name. Validate assignment stability, held-out decision prediction, intervention stability and separation from admitted packages. Descriptor failure closes or revises the measurement contract before repertoire search.

### Layer C — conditional repertoire discovery

Deterministic enumeration or QMC over the admitted representation is the default baseline. A quality-diversity method receives simulator budget only after a frozen matched-budget test shows decision value over that baseline.

Where admitted, the repertoire archive must:

- use the validated causal/trace descriptor rather than deck thickness or card-list distance alone;
- treat quality and descriptor membership as uncertain distributions;
- preserve deep/reserve candidates where noise can displace an elite;
- allocate rows only when archive membership or a scientific decision remains unresolved;
- keep discovery policies/seeds separate from confirmation; and
- never treat archive occupancy as P9 acceptance.

Surrogates, BO, RL, planning or learned descriptors are later escalations. Each earns authority through held-out recommendation value or a measured action/representation bottleneck at matched cost.

### Layer D — corrected confirmation and endpoint retention

Freeze the candidate set, product/simulator identity, descriptors, policies, contexts, CRN map, sample size, stopping rules and error budget. On untouched evidence:

- recompute performance and descriptor membership;
- allow apparent elites to move cell or fail;
- report apparent versus corrected repertoire coverage and quality;
- use statistically controlled ranking/selection within and across package claims;
- confirm reachability and package activation on the real reward/economy path; and
- run independent fresh local searches without archive protection.

The endpoint evidence must show several distinct high-performing basins/endpoints and quantify collapse/retention. A protected MAP-Elites cell, different deck list or candidate name is not C3 evidence.

### Layer E — product integration and exact-SHA certification

Research remains outside product truth. Promote at most one reproducible detector/candidate packet:

1. integrate current `main` once;
2. create one clean delivery branch;
3. apply only the selected minimal runtime/schema/content and detector packet;
4. run relevant exact-head review and scope-aware gates;
5. merge one ordinary PR;
6. run the final protected P9 protocol on the exact merged product/content identities; and
7. post the signed P9 receipt to #108.

Research scaffolding, caches, model state and opaque online policies do not ship.

## 5. Detector acceptance and hard guardrails

The replacement detector is admitted only after matched, stall-free causal mutations cover all seven directions:

1. one-route spike;
2. several separated viable routes;
3. flat overpowered/no-skill content;
4. selective RandomBuild regression;
5. aspect-identity reversal;
6. route-specific improvement without global-difficulty movement; and
7. global-difficulty movement without route differentiation.

The existing #421 thresholds remain unchanged:

- all seven directions and both critical contrasts pass on held-out evidence;
- held-out directional accuracy >= `0.85`;
- held-out rank Spearman >= `0.70`;
- balanced-accuracy gain over the old nine-cell detector >= `0.10`;
- rank gain >= `0.15`;
- mechanism-activation prediction R-squared >= `0.50`;
- local-collapse/retention accuracy >= `0.85`;
- probe-bootstrap sign agreement >= `0.90`; and
- every hard guardrail is green.

Package admission still requires at least two independently validated packages per aspect, including or improving Ashwarden `hand-size-payoff`, with held-out complementarity, functional separation, policy sensitivity, real-economy reachability, reproducibility and no broad global/RandomBuild movement.

C2 RandomBuild separation, aspect identity, the Vow-5 ceiling, deterministic RNG/replay, save/internal-ID compatibility, zero added stalls/errors, no material duration regression and content validity are constraints, not weighted objectives.

## 6. Continuous certification for later cards and expansions

Once the initial detector and package registry are admitted, every balance-relevant change receives one deterministic **P9 impact classification**. The change issue/PR owns its delta receipt; it does not create a P9 successor issue.

| Class | Typical change | Minimum P9 action |
|---|---|---|
| `D0` | docs, copy or presentation with no gameplay/selection effect | carry P9 evidence; record no-impact classification |
| `D1` | refactor, telemetry or harness change claimed semantics-preserving | replay identity, metamorphic and affected instrumentation tests; re-run any evidence whose observation identity changed |
| `D2` | scalar/cost/rarity/acquisition adjustment inside admitted grammar/package | affected-package causal/reachability panel, guardrails, detector prediction and corrected confirmation slice; full P9 only if a control band or dependency is crossed |
| `D3` | new card/relic/effect using admitted grammar | canonical classification, equivalence/decomposition screen, constrained interaction coverage, affected-package validation and delta confirmation; enter Layer A if it introduces a new topology |
| `D4` | expansion, encounter/economy/reward or policy-context change | update the context model, constrained combinatorial/sequence coverage and all affected package claims; revalidate detector if context or policy support changes materially |
| `D5` | detector/descriptor semantics, simulator oracle, RNG, save/ID contract or primary gameplay subsystem change | full system revalidation and a new exact-SHA P9 receipt |

A deterministic claim-evidence graph owns carry-forward. At minimum it versions:

- acceptance contract;
- detector implementation/thresholds;
- descriptor and canonicalisation rules;
- package registry;
- runtime/simulator oracle;
- policy grammar;
- context distribution;
- content/reward/economy identities;
- seed/cohort map; and
- evidence packet hashes.

A change invalidates only claims reachable from its changed dependencies. Carry-forward is explicit evidence, not an assumption. Any unknown dependency, ambiguous classification, failed metamorphic relation, descriptor drift or control-band breach escalates to the next class and fails closed.

## 7. Required delta receipt

Every future balance-relevant delivery records:

```text
P9 change class:
Changed semantic and identity hashes:
Affected claims/packages/contexts:
Carried evidence and why it remains valid:
Invalidated evidence:
Metamorphic/combinatorial/causal gates run:
Fresh identities opened:
Decision and residual risk:

```

The receipt belongs in the normal delivery PR or its issue. Research rows remain in the programme ledger/cache. No separate issue is created per candidate, batch or negative result.

## 9. Operating rules

- #421 remains one active programme; no research-successor chain.
- One accountable owner and one mutable research ledger/cache.
- Freeze grammar, evidence inputs, factors, estimands, identities, budgets and stop rules before rows.
- Deterministic code owns enumeration, execution, caching, fitting and stop/go.
- Model judgement may define a bounded grammar and competing hypotheses before freeze; it may not adapt candidates after outcomes without a separately preregistered decision.
- Use the cheapest gate capable of falsifying the current claim.
- Preserve every valid negative and stop when decision value is exhausted.
- Never weaken acceptance because implementation or a method fails.
- Promote once, through one clean integration boundary.
