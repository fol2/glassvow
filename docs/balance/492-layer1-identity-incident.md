qualified M4 Mac mini and emitted 21,760 rows under
`/private/tmp/glassvow-492-e501b520/layer1`. Its derived decision stopped every
candidate because the new strict aspect-identity check read the valid-cell
occupancy floor rather than the aspect-level top route.

That interpretation was not executable evidence of “Dusk remains shatter-led
and Ash smolder-led”. At this fidelity, the Dusk valid-cell floor contained only

invalid or non-viable occupancy bucket as the aspect leader was a protocol
implementation error, not a candidate failure.

interval, promotion or stop from them may enter the #492 result, and no row is
inherited into the replacement run. The raw directory remains intact as the
incident record. No mini-CEM, audit, acceptance/reserve seed or root 215/216 was
opened.
