`t1-c036` still fired all eight changed packages, cleared C2, strict identity
and Vow-5, and had fewer stalls. Those facts do not rescue the response: it lost
the Layer-1 point improvement as fidelity increased and was worse on both C1
components. The frozen rule therefore stopped it before Layer 3.

The strong bar required at least three grids at `3 / 4` and the remaining grid
at `2 / 3`, plus strengthened non-dominant thin/mid cells. Layer 2's best grid
was only `2 / 0` on the incumbent and `1 / 1` on `t1-c036`. Spending Layer 3 or
mini-CEM after the sole promotion reversed would not satisfy the registered
campaign.
