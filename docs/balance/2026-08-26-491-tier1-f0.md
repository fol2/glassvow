## Mechanism audit

Every package **fired** on every candidate. None is unreachable at this
fidelity. Exposure comes from recorded events, not names.

| Package | Exposure | Use | What it did |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dusk starter attrition | starters always drawn | Eclipse Slash / Cracked fire | High is a weak C1 helper; low is worse. Does not open a non-shatter Dusk route on its own. |
| Ash starter thin/sustain | starters always drawn | Ashbite / Smother fire | High raises thin/mid win rate slightly and **worsens** v1 C1 (+0.32). It fattens the Ash Smolder top. |
| Neutral cycle | Flicker / Glasstep / Tinder offered and played | cycle draws fire | Low is a small identity-clean C1 gain (`t1-c005`). High is not. |
| Removal economy | shops, Shrine, Mirror all reached | removals fire | High (cheaper removal) is a modest C1 gain on the anchor but adds stalls. Low (dearer removal) does not thin into a winning route. |
| Ashcloud route | Ashcloud offered and played | Smolder from Ashcloud fires | High is roughly neutral on C1; low is worse. Not a new Ash route. |
| Dusk non-shatter (Refract) | Refract offered, drawn, played | Ward and draw fire | **High is the cleanest single-package C1 move** (`t1-c012`). Identity holds. |
| Smolder relic stack | Core / Coal / Choir reached | relics trigger, Choir plays | High helps mid win rate and C1 a little; low is worse. It amplifies the existing Ash Smolder attractor. |
| Boss energy (Hollow Crown) | offered, picked, owned | extra energy and Max-HP loss fire | **Low (0 energy) is the worst main effect** (+0.60 C1). High helps mid win rate and C1. Not a thin route; it is an energy route. |

Thin occupancy rose on all-low vectors (`t1-c037` +620 thin runs) because weaker
cards fall under the frozen decline threshold. Thin win rate stayed ≈2%. That is
not a thin *route*; it is more losing thin decks. Mid win rate is the occupancy
signal that actually moves (baseline 0.31; `bossEnergyRoute=high` mean 0.35;
`t1-c040` 0.36).

## Valid-cell floor

The frozen #490 cell-validity rule (20 distinct policies **and** 400 runs) is
almost never met at 128 policies × 8 seeds (1,024 runs per grid, ~114 per
lean:thick cell). Most grids have **zero** valid cells. The one valid cell on
`t1-c000` Dusk V5 is `attrition:thin` at **0%** win rate — a populated loss
bucket, not a top route.

Contract promotion therefore returned an **empty** eligible shortlist:
`breadth-not-credible` on all 48 (valid-cell within-10 / viable cannot move),
plus Vow-5 fail-closed-when-missing on 32 candidates whose Ash V5 grid has no
valid cell. That is a fidelity finding, not a “packages do nothing” finding.

v1 all-cell C1a/C1b (the #457 proxies) still move. Those, plus mid win rate and
identity, are what #492 should race.

## Shortlist for #492

Contract-eligible set: **empty**. `racingSet` in
[`data/491/tidy.json`](data/491/tidy.json) is the four-candidate race below.
`breadthPareto` is valid-cell occupancy at a floor that cannot see thin/mid;
it is not the race.

Racing set, kept as distinct trade-offs:

1. **`t1-c012`** — only `duskNonShatter=high`. Identity-clean C1. First package
   to race.
2. **`t1-c036`** — best v1 C1. Dusk V0 smolder:fat watch; do not treat as
   identity-preserving.
3. **`t1-c040`** — Dusk V0 top is smolder:**mid**. The only candidate whose top
   cell is not fat. All-high except removal. Race as the mid-route contrast.
4. **`t1-c005`** — `neutralCycle=low` only. Small identity-clean C1. Optional
   fourth.

Do not race `bossEnergyRoute=low`, all-low thinning, or Ash-starter high as if
they were thin-route tools.

## Is the first registry worth racing?

**Yes, as a four-candidate race, not as another 48-row grind.** The eight
packages all fire. One of them (Refract high) moves identity-clean C1. Hollow
Crown high moves mid win rate. Ash-starter high and 0-energy Crown do the wrong
thing.

The registry does **not** need a new family for F1. It needs a bounded fidelity
redesign for the *valid-cell* gate: 400 runs at 128×8 cannot see thin/mid cells.
#492 should raise landscape seeds or policy count on the shortlist, still using
the frozen #215 axes, and must not spend CEM until that race says C1a still
cannot reach within-10 count 3 on Dusk.
