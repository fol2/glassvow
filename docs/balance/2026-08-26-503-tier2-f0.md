## Question

Across the complete four-knob block/disruption factorial, do any legal
counter-profile enemy settings measurably broaden thin/mid and non-dominant
routes relative to exact `t2-c000` (s009 content + baseline mobs)?

weak, frail or vulnerable. It never means cleanse, purge, dispel or status
removal.

## Mechanism audit

Selected profiles **gravewarden / shellback** (block pair) and **waylayer /
watcherEye** (disruption pair) were encountered and fired in every grid on every
candidate. None is unreachable at this fidelity. Disruption events kept target
`player` and an allowed status; authored amounts followed the intensity knob.
Attribution therefore holds: a proxy movement, were one to appear, could be
credited to these profiles.

On `t2-c000` landscape exposure/firing (1,024 runs per grid):

| Grid | Exposed | Fired | Firing rate | Block events | Disruption events |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dusk V0 | 952 | 918 | 0.964 | 372 | 2,545 |
| Dusk V5 | 919 | 886 | 0.964 | 419 | 2,667 |
| Ash V0 | 995 | 993 | 0.998 | 774 | 3,277 |
| Ash V5 | 957 | 956 | 0.999 | 863 | 3,318 |

Identity load rules hold on all 81. There is no identity-reversal. All-cell Dusk
V0 nevertheless tops **smolder:fat** on the baseline (Ash tops attrition:fat).
That is an all-cell ranking observation, not a valid-cell identity fault: Dusk V0
has **zero** occupied valid cells at 128×8.

## Valid-cell floor

The frozen #490/#508 cell-validity rule (20 distinct policies **and** 400 runs)
is almost never met at 128 policies × 8 seeds. Baseline occupied valid cells are
0, 1, 0, 1 on Dusk V0 / Dusk V5 / Ash V0 / Ash V5. Valid-cell C1a/C1b therefore
cannot move to the promotion thresholds (within-10 count 3, viable count 4).
Contract promotion returned an **empty** eligible shortlist:
`breadth-not-credible` on all 81. That is a fidelity finding, not “profiles do
not fire”.

Thin win rate stays ≈2% on the baseline. Extra thin occupancy, where it occurs,
is more losing thin decks, not a thin *route*.

## Factorial effects

Paired seed-block bootstrap, B = 10,000, seed 508, jointly resampling the 32
control seeds and 8 landscape seeds across all 81 candidates. Point estimates are
the exact orthogonal-polynomial OLS on the complete factorial. An interval that
crosses zero is not a finding. **No C1a or C1b main effect or pairwise
interaction excludes zero.**

Findings that do exclude zero are occupancy, stall, firing and C2-margin shifts,
not new winning thin/mid cells. The largest named movements:

| Term | Response | Estimate | 95% interval | Reading |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| `blockTempoTrade` linear | thin occupancy | −0.0076 | entirely below 0 | high durability/tempo takes mass out of thin |
| `blockTempoTrade` linear | C2 margin | −0.027 | entirely below 0 | high durability slightly compresses the random-arm gap |
| `disruptionIntensity` linear | stalls | +0.26 | entirely above 0 | more disruption adds landscape stalls |
| `disruptionIntensity` linear | mid win rate | −0.0070 | entirely below 0 | more disruption makes mid decks worse, not better |
| `disruptionTempoTrade` linear | thin occupancy | +0.0090 | entirely above 0 | longer disruption window populates thin |
| `disruptionTempoTrade` linear | stalls | +0.69 | entirely above 0 | more disruption cycles add stalls |

Those are global difficulty / occupancy shifts. They do not strengthen a Dusk
thin/mid non-dominant cell **and** an Ash thin/mid non-dominant cell in the
promotion sense. `blockMitigation` linear only moves C2 margin. No interaction
creates a C1 finding.

## Does the four-entry package merit racing?

**No.** The four knobs fire and are attributable. They change occupancy, stalls
and C2 margin. They do not create credible paired C1a/C1b breadth in at least one
Dusk grid and one Ash grid, and they do not strengthen thin/mid non-dominant
cells on both aspects. The package is already insufficient for #504 at this
fixed F0 rectangle.

A later ticket that wants to re-open enemy ecology should first raise landscape
fidelity on `t2-c000` alone (more seeds or policies, still frozen #215 axes)
until valid cells exist in every grid. It must not spend F1/CEM seeds 12300–13399
or audit 14000–14199 on an empty racing set.
