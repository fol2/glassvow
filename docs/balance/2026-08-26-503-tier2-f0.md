## Question

Across the complete four-knob block/disruption factorial, do any legal
counter-profile enemy settings measurably broaden thin/mid and non-dominant
routes relative to exact `t2-c000` (s009 content + baseline mobs)?

weak, frail or vulnerable. It never means cleanse, purge, dispel or status
removal.

## Mechanism audit

watcherEye** (disruption pair) were encountered and fired in every grid on every
candidate. None is unreachable at this fidelity. Disruption events kept target
`player` and an allowed status; authored amounts followed the intensity knob.
Attribution therefore holds: a proxy movement, were one to appear, could be
credited to these profiles.

## Valid-cell floor

The frozen #490/#508 cell-validity rule requires 20 distinct policies **and**
400 runs per cell. Each grid contains only `128 policies × 8 seeds = 1,024`
rows. Three 400-run valid cells require at least 1,200 rows and four require at
least 1,600 rows, before the distinct-policy condition is considered. The
original C1 promotion target (within-10 count 3 and viable count 4) was therefore
mathematically unreachable at this rectangle.

Baseline occupied valid cells are 0, 1, 0, 1 on Dusk V0 / Dusk V5 / Ash V0 /
Ash V5. Contract promotion returned an **empty** eligible shortlist:
`breadth-not-credible` on all 81. That is a protocol-fidelity finding, not
evidence that the profiles do not fire or cannot affect route breadth.

Thin win rate stays ≈2% on the baseline. Extra thin occupancy, where it occurs,
is more losing thin decks, not a thin *route*.

## Factorial effects

Paired seed-block bootstrap, B = 10,000, seed 508, jointly resampling the 32
control seeds and 8 landscape seeds across all 81 candidates. Point estimates are
the exact orthogonal-polynomial OLS on the complete factorial. An interval that
crosses zero is not a finding. **No observed C1a or C1b main effect or pairwise
interaction excludes zero within this rectangle.** Because the promotion target
was unreachable, this result does not establish the absence of a route-breadth
effect at a different fidelity.

Findings that do exclude zero are occupancy, stall, firing and C2-margin shifts.
The largest named movements:

| Term | Response | Estimate | 95% interval | Reading |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| `blockTempoTrade` linear | thin occupancy | −0.0076 | entirely below 0 | high durability/tempo takes mass out of thin |
| `blockTempoTrade` linear | C2 margin | −0.027 | entirely below 0 | high durability slightly compresses the random-arm gap |
| `disruptionIntensity` linear | stalls | +0.26 | entirely above 0 | more disruption adds landscape stalls |
| `disruptionIntensity` linear | mid win rate | −0.0070 | entirely below 0 | more disruption makes mid decks worse, not better |
| `disruptionTempoTrade` linear | thin occupancy | +0.0090 | entirely above 0 | longer disruption window populates thin |
| `disruptionTempoTrade` linear | stalls | +0.69 | entirely above 0 | more disruption cycles add stalls |

Those are the measured effects: occupancy, stalls, mid win rate and C2 margin.
The rectangle did not observe a valid-cell promotion outcome, but it cannot
separate absence of route breadth from insufficient rows for the frozen valid-
cell target. Among the reported responses, `blockMitigation` linear moves only
C2 margin, and no observed C1 interaction excludes zero.

all-cell smolder/attrition tops as if they were thin-route tools.

## Does the four-entry package merit racing?

**Not from this evidence.** The four knobs fire and are attributable. The
measured effects are the occupancy, stall, mid-win-rate and C2-margin shifts
reported above. The original C1 promotion target was mathematically unreachable,
so this screen cannot support the broader claim that the package does or does not
create route breadth.

Operationally, the contract-eligible `racingSet` is empty, so #504 has no
candidate to race and must not spend F1/CEM seeds 12300–13399 or audit seeds
14000–14199.
